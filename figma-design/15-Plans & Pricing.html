Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our plans

Can I switch plans anytime?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and billing is prorated.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and local Dominican Republic payment methods including bank transfers.

Is there a free trial for paid plans?

Yes! We offer a 14-day free trial for Pro and Elite plans. No credit card required to start.

What's included in the DR Market access?

Real-time quotes from BVRD (Bolsa de Valores de República Dominicana), historical data, company fundamentals, and local market news.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, you can cancel anytime. Your access continues until the end of your billing period with no additional charges.
